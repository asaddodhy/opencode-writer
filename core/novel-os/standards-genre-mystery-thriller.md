# Mystery and Thriller Style Guide

## Genre Characteristics

### Plot Structure
- Establish the mystery or threat early in the story
- Plant clues fairly throughout the narrative
- Build suspense through pacing and revelation timing
- Ensure a logical resolution that satisfies reader expectations

### Pacing and Tension
- Maintain consistent forward momentum
- Use short chapters and cliffhangers strategically
- Balance action scenes with investigation and discovery
- Escalate stakes throughout the story

### Information Management
- Control what readers know versus what characters know
- Use red herrings sparingly and fairly
- Reveal information at optimal dramatic moments
- Maintain reader engagement through strategic withholding

## Character Development

### Protagonist Characteristics
- Give the detective or protagonist compelling personal stakes
- Develop investigative skills appropriate to character background
- Create believable motivation for pursuing the mystery
- Show character growth through solving the case

### Antagonist Development
- Create logical, well-motivated villains
- Provide clear but not obvious antagonist motivations
- Make antagonist actions consistent with their goals
- Avoid cartoonish evil; ground antagonists in realistic psychology

## Technical Elements

### Clue Placement
- Introduce clues naturally within scenes
- Make clues discoverable by both character and reader
- Ensure clues are significant to the resolution
- Avoid coincidental or convenient clue discovery

### Dialogue in Investigation
- Use dialogue to reveal information and character
- Create natural-sounding interrogation scenes
- Show character relationships through conversation
- Advance plot through character interactions

## Suspense Techniques

### Building Tension
- Use time pressure and deadlines effectively
- Create multiple layers of threat or mystery
- Employ misdirection without cheating the reader
- Balance revelation with new questions

### Chapter Structure
- End chapters at moments of discovery or danger
- Use scene breaks to control pacing
- Alternate between action and investigation scenes
- Maintain reader investment through strategic revelation
